FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Short answers about accounts, privacy, languages and price.

Do I need to create an account to use PayChase?

No. PayChase works without an account or sign-up. Your invoices and clients are stored on your phone.

Does PayChase work offline?

Yes. You can add invoices, see what is overdue and prepare reminders without an internet connection. You only need a connection when WhatsApp or your email app actually sends the message.

Which languages are the reminder templates in?

English, Roman Urdu, Urdu, Hindi and Arabic. Each comes in Friendly, Professional and Firm tones, and you can edit any message before sending.

Will my client know I used an app?

No. Reminders are sent from your own WhatsApp or email, so they look like a normal message from you.

Is PayChase free?

PayChase is free to download from Google Play. Optional upgrades are available inside the app.
