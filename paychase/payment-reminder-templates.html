English payment reminder templates

Stage 1

Before the due date

Send about 3 days before the invoice is due.

Friendly

Hi [Client name], hope you're well! Just a quick heads-up that invoice [Invoice #] for [Amount] is due on [Due date]. Here are the payment details to make it easy: [Payment details] Thanks so much! [Your name]

Professional

Hello [Client name], This is a courtesy reminder that invoice [Invoice #] for [Amount] is due on [Due date]. Payment details: [Payment details] Please let me know if you need anything from me to process it. Kind regards, [Your name]

Firm

Hello [Client name], Invoice [Invoice #] for [Amount] is due on [Due date]. Please arrange payment by that date using: [Payment details] Please confirm once it has been scheduled. Regards, [Your name]

Stage 2

Due today

Send on the morning of the due date.

Friendly

Hi [Client name], just a friendly reminder that invoice [Invoice #] for [Amount] is due today. If it's already on its way, thank you! Payment details: [Payment details] [Your name]

Professional

Hello [Client name], Invoice [Invoice #] for [Amount] is due today, [Due date]. Could you confirm that payment has been scheduled? Payment details: [Payment details] Thank you, [Your name]

Firm

Hello [Client name], Invoice [Invoice #] for [Amount] is due for payment today. Please make the payment today using [Payment details] and send me the confirmation. Regards, [Your name]

Stage 3

A few days late

Send 3 to 7 days after the due date.

Friendly

Hi [Client name], I hope all is well. I noticed invoice [Invoice #] for [Amount] (due [Due date]) hasn't come through yet. It may have slipped through the cracks, so could you take a look when you get a chance? Thanks! [Your name]

Professional

Hello [Client name], My records show that invoice [Invoice #] for [Amount], due on [Due date], is now [X] days overdue. Could you let me know when I can expect payment? If there is any issue with the invoice, I'm happy to help sort it out. Kind regards, [Your name]

Firm

Hello [Client name], Invoice [Invoice #] for [Amount] was due on [Due date] and is now overdue. Please arrange payment within the next 3 working days, or reply with a confirmed payment date. Payment details: [Payment details] Regards, [Your name]

Stage 4

Two weeks late, work paused

Send around 14 days late, if you are pausing further work.

Friendly

Hi [Client name], I'm following up on invoice [Invoice #] for [Amount], which is now two weeks overdue. I'd love to keep the project moving, but I'll need to pause new work until this invoice is settled. As soon as it's paid, I'll pick things straight back up. Is anything holding it up that I can help with? [Your name]

Professional

Hello [Client name], Invoice [Invoice #] for [Amount] (due [Due date]) is now 14 days overdue and I haven't received an update. I am pausing further work on [Project] until the outstanding balance is cleared, and will resume as soon as payment arrives. If paying in instalments would help, please reply and we can agree a plan. Kind regards, [Your name]

Firm

Hello [Client name], Invoice [Invoice #] for [Amount] is now more than two weeks overdue. Work on [Project] is paused from today and will restart once the full balance is received. Please send payment to [Payment details], or reply today with a confirmed payment date. Regards, [Your name]

Stage 5

Final reminder

Send around 30 days late, after earlier reminders have gone unanswered.

Friendly

Hi [Client name], I've reached out a few times about invoice [Invoice #] for [Amount], which is now [X] days overdue. I value working with you and would like to sort this out together. Could you pay by [Date], or let me know what's possible? A partial payment or a payment plan is fine. Thank you, [Your name]

Professional

Hello [Client name], This is my final reminder about invoice [Invoice #] for [Amount], which was due on [Due date] and is now [X] days overdue. I sent earlier reminders on [Dates]. Please arrange payment by [Date], or contact me to agree a payment plan. If I don't hear back, I will need to review my options for recovering the amount, which I would much prefer to avoid. Kind regards, [Your name]

Firm

Hello [Client name], Final reminder: invoice [Invoice #] for [Amount] is now [X] days overdue, despite reminders sent on [Dates]. Please pay the full balance to [Payment details] by [Date], or reply with a written payment plan. After this I will look at other ways to recover the amount, though I would much rather we settle it directly. Regards, [Your name]

Tips that get invoices paid faster

  • Always include the basics: invoice number, amount, due date and how to pay. Make paying the easiest thing to do.
  • Ask a question. "Could you confirm the payment date?" gets more replies than "Please pay."
  • Escalate slowly. Most late payments are forgotten, not refused. Save the firm tone for when friendly messages are ignored.
  • Only pause work if you mean it, and check your contract allows it.
  • Keep a record of every reminder you send. It helps if you ever need to take things further.
  • Never threaten or harass. Don't contact a client's family or employer, post about them publicly, or make threats you can't or won't follow through on.

FAQ

Questions

How many payment reminders should I send?

A simple sequence of five works well: a heads-up before the due date, a note on the due date, a nudge a few days late, a firmer message around two weeks late, and a final reminder around 30 days late.

Should I send payment reminders on WhatsApp or email?

Use whichever channel your client actually replies on. WhatsApp often gets faster replies; email gives you a cleaner written record. Many freelancers send on WhatsApp and keep a copy by email.

How do I ask for payment without sounding rude?

Start friendly, assume a mistake rather than bad intent, include the invoice number, amount and payment details, and ask a clear question such as when payment will be made. Get firmer only when earlier reminders are ignored.
