Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This policy covers the PayChase Android app (package com.paychase.invoice.followup.payment.reminder) and this website. In short: your invoices and clients stay on your phone, there is no PayChase account, and we do not run any servers that receive your data.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter in PayChase is stored only on your device: your business profile, clients, invoices, payments, promises, reminder history, logo and settings. We cannot see it. If you delete the app or use "Delete all data", it is gone. Backups are files you create and save yourself; we never receive them.

Permissions the app uses

  • Camera and photos: only when you choose "Scan an invoice". The image is read on your phone with Google ML Kit text recognition and is not uploaded.
  • Contacts picker: only when you tap "Pick from Contacts". You choose one contact; the app gets just that contact's name, phone or email. It cannot read your whole address book.
  • Notifications: to remind you which clients to chase. They are scheduled on your phone.
  • Internet: for ads, Google Play purchases, and opening WhatsApp, email or your phone app when you send a reminder.

Messages you send

When you send a reminder, PayChase opens WhatsApp, your email app or your phone dialer with the message filled in. You send it from that app, and that app's own privacy policy applies.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, and basic device and app-usage information, and may use it for personalised ads where the law allows. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Google settings. Pro users do not see ads. See how Google uses data in ads.

Purchases (Google Play)

PayChase Pro is sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or billing details. The app only learns whether your Google account owns Pro. Manage or cancel subscriptions in the Google Play Store under Payments and subscriptions.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or trackers, and loads nothing from other servers. The calculators run in your browser and nothing you type is sent anywhere.

Children

PayChase is a business tool and is not aimed at children under 13.

Your rights

Because your PayChase data is only on your phone, you can view, export, correct or delete it yourself at any time in the app. For data held by Google for ads or purchases, use your Google account settings.
